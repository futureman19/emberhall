Day 1 · Morning

EMBERHALL

You are a body in a vale that does not wait. The old hall still stands. Timber, red cloth, a chest, a road into the woods. Chop. Hunt. Sell. Steal if you must. Other people have errands of their own.

How the vale works

  • Click ground to walk. The camera follows you.
  • Click a tree, rock, animal, or person when you are next to it.
  • Wield a hatchet to chop, a pick to mine, a blade to fight.
  • Skills rise by using them. Nothing is purchased.
  • The chest at the hall is a bank. Death leaves your pack on the road.
  • Hurt a person and the guard will remember. Night belongs to wolves.
  • The vale has height. Hall sits on a terrace. Steps drop to the road. The north woods climb to a ridge. Cliffs turn you around; stairs do not.

You are not the guildmaster of a spreadsheet. You are Fen Moss, and the hall is only home if you return to it.
