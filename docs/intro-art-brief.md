# Emberhall Intro — Artist Brief (5 story panels)

**What this is:** Emberhall is a gentle fantasy village game. New players see a
short first-run cinematic: five full-screen story cards, each with one painted
panel behind the text. You're painting those five panels.

## Shared style guide (applies to all five)

- **Format:** 16:9 landscape. Deliver ≥ 1920×1080 PNG (displayed full-screen,
  downscaled to 960×540).
- **Style:** cozy voxel diorama meets storybook minimalism. Simple **blocky,
  geometric shapes** with **flat muted colors**. Soft gradients allowed in
  skies only. **No** outlines, painterly texture, neon, or anime exaggeration.
- **People:** simple boxy figures — rectangular head, block torso, short legs,
  **blank faces** (the game's figures have no facial features; keep it that
  way so the art matches the world).
- **Text zone:** keep the **upper third calm and uncluttered** — story text
  overlays there (white serif). Focal action belongs in the lower two-thirds.
- **Palette (lifted from the game — please match, don't improvise):**

  | Use | Hex |
  |---|---|
  | skin / pale stone | `#c9c3b6` |
  | hair / dark wood | `#3a322c` / `#2e241c` |
  | rust / terracotta | `#a85a42` |
  | ranger green | `#6a7a48` |
  | wheat / path tan | `#c9a36a` |
  | steel / stone grey | `#8a8680` |
  | mage purple | `#6a5a78` |
  | rogue grey | `#5a5a52` |
  | merchant gold | `#a88848` |
  | ghost-white (moon, fire core) | `#ece6d8` |
  | ember glow (windows, firelight) | `#e8b96a` |

## Panel 1 — "The Vale"

> *"Beyond the last road, the hills fold around a quiet vale. No maps name it.
> No king taxes it."*

- **Composition:** wide establishing shot at **dusk**. Layered rolling hills
  recede toward a purple sky; early stars prick through; a small blocky moon
  hangs low. A faint pale path winds down into the valley and vanishes —
  the way in.
- **Palette:** sky gradient deep indigo `#23202b` → violet `#3a3145` →
  `#6a5a78`, with one thin rust band `#a85a42` at the horizon; hills darken
  toward the viewer (`#3a342e` → `#241f1c`).
- **Mood:** hush, discovery, the edge of the map.

## Panel 2 — "The Hall"

> *"At its heart stands Emberhall — old stone, warm windows, and a door that
> has been waiting for someone."*

- **Composition:** **night**. A stout stone hall, centered, filling the middle
  distance: two crenellated square towers flanking a gabled main block.
  Windows glow warm ember against the blue-black night; a ribbon of chimney
  smoke curls into a starry sky. The door is dark, centered, shut — for now.
- **Palette:** night sky `#1c1a22` → `#342e36`; stone `#565049` with shaded
  faces `#423d38`; roof `#33291f`; window glow `#e8b96a` with soft halos
  pooling on the walls.
- **Mood:** sanctuary, age, welcome held in reserve.

## Panel 3 — "The Folk"

> *"Ranger, warrior, mage, rogue, merchant — the folk of the vale keep their
> own counsel. They are waiting to see who you are."*

- **Composition:** **night campfire**. Five boxy figures gathered around a
  crackling fire — green ranger, brown warrior, purple-**hooded** mage, grey
  rogue, gold merchant (chest colors from the palette table; blank faces).
  One figure sits beyond the flames, half-silhouetted. Sparks rise into the
  dark.
- **Palette:** near-black warm darks all around; fire built in three layers —
  rust `#a85a42` outer, wheat `#c9a36a` middle, ghost-white `#ece6d8` core —
  with an ember `#e8b96a` glow pool on the ground and figures' firelit sides.
- **Mood:** fellowship, quiet judgment, warmth you haven't earned yet.

## Panel 4 — "The Land"

> *"The land gives to those who learn it. Timber and ore, field and flame, gem
> and hide — patience makes them yours."*

- **Composition:** **golden dawn**. A tree with a blocky layered canopy, a
  grey rock outcrop, and rows of wheat stitching the foreground; one small
  figure stands among it all, looking out; two birds in a brightening sky.
- **Palette:** sky `#efe3c4` → `#d9b98a` → `#b8906a` with a warm pale sun low
  on the horizon; field greens-browns `#60583e`–`#6f6250`; wheat `#c9a36a`;
  canopy `#6a7a48`; stone `#8a8680`.
- **Mood:** plenty, patience, morning of the world.

## Panel 5 — "Your Arrival"

> *"The door is open. Who walks through it?"*

- **Composition:** **sunrise, from behind the viewer's shoulder**. A single
  small figure stands on a pale path that runs straight to the hall's **open
  door**, warm light spilling out onto the path toward them. Long soft
  shadow behind the figure. This is the emotional payoff — the threshold.
- **Palette:** brightest of the five — sunrise golds and creams; the hall in
  warm silhouette; the door glow `#e8b96a` is the brightest point in the
  whole series.
- **Mood:** invitation, hope, the story about to start.
