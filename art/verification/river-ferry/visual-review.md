# Visual review — Reedwake Crossing

Reviewed actual Blender hero and contact-sheet renders plus actual final desktop and mobile browser screenshots, not mockups.

Initial review found unsupported-looking water cargo and an ambiguous landing/hull overlap. Cargo was relocated onto raised bank; the wreck was moved outward, yielding a saved-source X separation of 0.2011578083 units. A removable submerged silt shoal was added and the front reed patch moved away from the hull. Initial images remain in `review-before/`.

Strict saved-source bank-envelope checks then found the winch crank and bank bollard crosspin extending beyond the conservative inland envelope. Both instances were moved inland; assertions were retained unchanged. Failed audit logs remain alongside `source-audit-accepted.log`. Final source/structure/GLTFLoader/browser audits passed after regeneration and remeasurement.

Final presentation: recognizable tapered open hull with exposed ribs and missing deck/strakes; distinct intact and broken dock modules; coherent warm timber and muted reed palette; readable raised land/opaque water boundary; cargo visibly on land; clear wreck-to-dock gap. Contact-sheet headings, eight cards and IDs fit. Desktop and mobile pages show real GLBs with no clipping, horizontal overflow or console errors. Small details are intentionally less readable at phone scale; the full module viewer remains available.

The upper bollard pair is deliberately mounted on the dock, not misplaced bank hardware. Only the foreground bank pair is required inland. The winch is abandoned, with an attached loose rope end, so no working cable to the wreck is claimed. Boat flooring and hull openings are intentional wreck damage. Simplified opaque water contact remains a stylized limitation, not accepted flotation or physics. No operational ferry claim.
