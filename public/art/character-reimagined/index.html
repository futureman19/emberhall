THE DESIGN

Small, but unmistakably you.

A shaped jaw and brows carry expression. Swept locks, a woven braid and cropped waves replace a generic cap. Tailored collars, split hems, stitched boots and thumbed hands give the same short adventurer silhouette more craft.

THE BOUNDARY

A proposal, not a replacement.

Current model is reconstructed from the actual installed character GLB with default colors, face, belt and idle anchors. Studio lighting is shared, not an in-game screenshot. New models have rigid part pivots—not a skinned rig. Equipment, creator recoloring, ghost effects and gameplay animation have not been integrated or tested.
